About CareTrack AI

Problem

Senior citizen trusts manage many residents, but maintaining individual healthcare records manually is difficult.

Solution

CareTrack AI helps caregivers store resident details, monitor health updates, manage medicines, and identify health risks.

Future Scope

Future versions can include real AI models, hospital integration, wearable devices, and family communication.
